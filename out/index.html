Fit

When this is the right hire

A fit when

  • The same job happens every week
  • The data and tools already exist
  • Someone inside will own it after handover
  • You want it running, not presented

Not a fit when

  • You only want to “add ChatGPT”
  • There is no workflow — only a vague interest in AI
  • You need a research lab, not a system in production
  • Nobody will take it after handover

Process

Name the job. Ship the system.

  1. 01

    Work audit

    Name the jobs that should not be manual — and which ones can actually run.

  2. 02

    Design the system

    Data, tools, human checkpoints, failure modes, and what “done” looks like.

  3. 03

    Build and deploy

    Product, backend, models or retrieval, infra — in your cloud, in your tools.

  4. 04

    Handoff

    Source, runbooks, evals. You keep the system. You don’t keep a contractor on the critical path.

What you leave with

A running system. Not a deck.

  • A running system in your cloud or account
  • Source you own
  • Docs and runbooks
  • Eval checks for quality, cost, and the ugly cases
